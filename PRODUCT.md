# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Tech recruiters and engineering managers hiring for platform, infrastructure and backend roles. They scan the portfolio site or the downloaded CV to decide whether to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio for Naphat Wattanarattanakul, infrastructure software engineer. It shows the work, experience and stack behind his job title and gets a qualified reader to email him. The site (English and Thai) is the long form; the CV PDF is the short form for application systems and hiring pipelines.

## Positioning
Builds self-service platforms for engineers and the AI agents working beside them: deployment workflows, infrastructure as code, CI quality gates, and MCP tools.

## Operating Context
- Site: static Astro build, English default with Thai at `/th/`.
- CV: generated by `npm run cv`, one A4 page per language (`public/cv.pdf`, `public/cv-th.pdf`), from the same data as the site (`src/data/site.ts`).
- CV readers often go through ATS parsers: real selectable text, a single logical reading order, no photo.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- All content lives in `src/data/site.ts`; the site and the CV must not drift apart.
- The CV must fit exactly one A4 page per language.

## Brand Commitments
- Swiss editorial minimalism, light by default, near-achromatic with one accent: KMUTT red `#C4291C`.
- Inter (Latin) and Anuphan (Thai), weights 400 and 500.

## Evidence on Hand
- Experience, projects, skills, education and certificates in `src/data/site.ts`; certificate images in `src/assets/certificates/`.
- No measured impact numbers yet (build time saved, teams onboarded). Do not invent them.

## Product Principles
- Necessary content only: every line must help a hiring decision.
- One source of truth for content across site and CV.
- Plain, factual claims; no invented metrics.
